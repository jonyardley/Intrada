The drill loop

Updated for spec v7 and the 2026-08-04 handover (decisions 18–19): machine listening is deferred, so A3 is redrawn around the tap-verdict — the user hears their own attempt and taps pass or fail against a countable criterion. There is no “app isn't sure” state; the earlier v5 uncertain screen is dropped, not degraded. A2 and A3 are full treatments (Phase 1); A1 and A4 stay rough passes (Phase 2a).

Assembled from the coach primitives in Design System · Components · Coach primitives. Worked example throughout: Strasbourg / St. Denis, campaign “restricted improv over Strasbourg by the 17th”.

Rule carried through
Nothing moves while you play, and no score exists before the rep ends.
Quiet ≠ faint
Ambient text is small and static, never low-contrast. inkFaint appears nowhere in the loop.
One target
One control size on the keys: the 66pt row (80 at AX5, 96 on iPad) shared by the verdict pair and the stuck target. Away from the keys: 62pt primary, 54pt secondary.

Block entry & session shape

Two surfaces fall out of the paper-teacher fortnight: the click now runs continuously through a block with one count-in at block entry only, so entry stops being a silent auto-advance and becomes a deliberate beat — and the press-start hero has to show the shape of the whole session, because “5 blocks · about 40 minutes” tells you nothing about what you agreed to.

Composes: identical to the block-1 state — only SegmentedProgress (two done), the OrientationStrip clock, and the kind tint differ. One layout, two data states, not two designs.

What these force on the system

Flagged rather than invented inline — both are compositions of existing parts, but two want promoting, and one wording rule is genuinely new.

New primitive · 1
BlockEntryCard — CardSurface + kind chip + minutes + serif intent line. Nothing in the kit currently pairs a kind chip with a serif sentence, and this composite appears six times a session. Promote once agreed.
New primitive · 2
PlanBlockRow — a non-tappable list row: tint bar, title, minutes, and deliberately no chevron. The existing session row is tappable and chevroned; reusing it here would promise a per-block screen that must not exist.
Content rule, not a token
The intent line is not CoachNote. CoachNote is three parts, once a session, at a boundary; this is one sentence, every block, supplied by the plan. Same serif treatment, different rule — worth stating in the DS so they don't merge.
No new tokens
Everything here is existing colour: cardFill, masteryFill indigo, the gold exercise pair, inkSecondary. The deferred lines use no colour of their own — a hairline and a smaller size do the demoting, because tinting them amber would read as a warning.
Open question for review
Does Skip need a confirm? Current call: no — skipping is cheap and reversible by the planner, and a confirm sheet is exactly the stall the entry card exists to remove.

AX5 · largest accessibility size
  • Required by the brief, and the honest test of the layout: this is also roughly what the default screen looks like from two metres.
  • What gives way, in order: the category chip and tune line drop first, then the orientation caps grow and the strip stays.
  • What never gives way: drill name, tempo, bar position, stuck target. Four things survive at any size.
  • The stuck target grows with type rather than staying fixed — at AX5 the user is likelier to need it.
What is deliberately absent
  • Any score, count or dot row. Layer 0 is silence.
  • A pause button. Stopping is the chevron — one gesture, no resident chrome.
  • Tabs and navigation. The drill screen is not inside the tab bar.
  • Notation or a bar map. Tempting, and it would pull the eyes off the keys — the one failure mode named in the brief.
  • Any microphone or MIDI status. Decision 18: there is nothing listening, and the screen doesn't apologise for it. The tap-verdict (A3) is the mechanism, and it explains itself.
Open question for the spec
“12:04 / 6:00” reads as block elapsed against the block's ceiling. If the strip should carry session elapsed instead, the clock moves under the strip and the header keeps only the block count.

Gate open — the handover to A4
  • Same shell, one word different. No confetti, no colour change — the reward is that the app moves on.

Why these are rough
Dashed frames and the badge above them mark a rough pass. They exist to prove three things and no more: the voice survives outside the drill loop, the equal-dignity pairing works at thumb scale, and the paper palette holds without a single number on screen.
What is knowingly undesigned: the something-else picker, the why-panel, the returning-after-a-gap variant (journey 7, Phase 2b), and any header affordance for the four pillars. A1 will be better designed after A2 and A3 have been used at a real piano — which is the reason the brief rough-passes it.

  • Same layout with the note removed, not a different screen. Silence must not look like something failed to load, so the fact (“3 clean at 120”) stays and only the coaching goes.

What this extended, and why

Everything below goes into Theme.swift and the design system together, per design-process.md §4.

Components · added to the DS
Six, all new: GateDots, RepVerdict, OrientationStrip, BeatPosition, StuckTarget, CoachNote. They are in Components · Coach primitives and are what make the eight deferred surfaces cheap: B3/B4 are CoachNote plus two equal buttons; the session-end narrative is CoachNote at length; the pipeline view is GateDots per stage.
Type · three new styles
IntradaFont.ambient — Inter medium 14, .subheadline. The orientation strip; meta at 12 is too small at a metre.
IntradaFont.drillTitle(_:42) — Serif semibold, .largeTitle. pageTitle's 32 default is a screen title, not a music-stand title.
IntradaFont.verdict(_:40) — Serif semibold, .title1, for the one-glance fact.
Spacing · one new step
IntradaSpacing.stage = 40 — the drill-screen rhythm. section (24) packs the drill screen tight enough that the eye scans it as a group rather than reading one thing; 40 is what separates the five facts into five glances.
Colour · nothing new
The engaging-refresh rep tokens already carry the loop: repClean* for the pass, repMissed* for the miss, masteryFill for gate dots, slotOutline for empties. The unsure state was the one gap, and it is answered by no tint at all rather than a new hue — cheaper, and truer, since it is a question.

One ban: inkFaint (2.9:1) is not usable anywhere in the loop.
Motion · one new rule, no new tokens
Nothing animates during play. The beat pip changes state with no transition and explicitly not pop. Between reps, the verdict uses pop and the newly-filled gate dot follows one fadeUpStagger later. fadeUp is suppressed on A2/A3 — a screen you return to forty times a session must not perform an entrance.
Resolved by decision 18 · GateDots is user-tapped, not machine-filled
The earlier RepCounter/GateDots collision dissolves with machine listening deferred: GateDots now fills on a tap-verdict, exactly like RepCounter always did — there is no separate machine-derived row to collide with it. GateDots stays the coach-loop name (it's the read-only display once a rep is tapped; RepCounter remains the manual counter for unmonitored play), and the “consecutive vs cumulative” question is settled cumulative per decision 17's self-correction argument, spelled out on the gate-open frame above.
New primitive · TapVerdict
The verdict pair (“Yes — clean” / “No — missed it”) reuses RepVerdict’s tinted treatment — green Clean, taupe Missed — grown to 66pt (96 on iPad) with Clean half again wider, so the coach loop and the manual counter speak one visual language. Goes into Components · Coach primitives alongside GateDots, OrientationStrip, BeatPosition, StuckTarget and CoachNote. The v5 “uncertain” third state is retired — decision 18 removed the state it represented.
