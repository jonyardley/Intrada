Journey A — compose from a lesson

A lesson hands over a piece and three exercises; the user wants to practise exactly those today, and everything still counts. Decision 11 — never a mode: the app opens prescribed, hero intact, and composing is reached for. Decision 19 — each new item gets one resolution: authored-node match, user node, or opaque target. Worked example: Alice in Wonderland plus Hanon №4, a stride pattern, and rubato work.

Assembled from Design System · Coach primitives (PressStartHero, PlanBlockRow, BlockEntryCard, GateDots, CoachAction, CoachNote). The play loop between A6 and A7 is not redrawn — see Drill Loop.dc.html.

A1 · where composition lives (open question 1)
  • The app opens prescribed, hero intact — nothing asks. The steer line sits directly under the hero, quiet weight, accent-coloured: reachable in one tap, never a footer secret like the old builder's link, never a mode chooser.
  • It is phrased as the user's own thought — “I know what I want to practise today” — not “Custom session”, because the built session is a steer on today, not an alternative app.
  • Tapping it opens a sheet over this screen (A2); the plan stays visible behind. Declining costs nothing and the hero is untouched.
